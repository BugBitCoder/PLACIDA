# 📊 Feedback Analysis (Week 4)

## Executive Summary
Overall reception of the Placida mental wellness app has been highly positive, with users appreciating the soothing UI and immediate accessibility of features. While core tools like breathing exercises and mood logging are well-received, the AI Chat needs further refinement to handle complex prompts more effectively.

## Feature Ratings
| Feature | Avg Rating /5 | Notes |
|---------|---------------|-------|
| Breathing | 4.5 | Highly praised for the UI and smooth animations. |
| Mood Logger | 4.2 | Simple and intuitive to use. |
| Journal | 4.1 | Users like the daily prompts. |
| Dashboard | 4.0 | Good overview, but wants more stats. |
| AI Chat | 3.8 | Needs better context handling and more crisis keywords. |

## Top 3 Praised Things
- 🎨 **Aesthetics & UI:** The dark mode glassmorphism design feels premium and calming.
- 🧘 **Breathing Exercises:** Very effective and visually relaxing.
- ⚡ **Performance:** Fast loading times and no lag during interactions.

## Top 3 Complaints
- 🤖 **AI Chatbot:** Responses sometimes feel repetitive or lack depth.
- 📊 **Dashboard Data:** Users want more historical data and graphs for their mood over time.
- 🧩 **Mobile Responsiveness:** A few overlapping issues on smaller screens.
