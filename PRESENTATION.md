# 🌿 Placida - 10-Minute Demo Presentation

## Slide 1: Problem
- **The Stigma**: Mental health remains a taboo topic in India, making it hard for people to seek help.
- **The Reality**: 1 in 7 Indians are affected by mental health issues (According to NIMHANS statistics).
- **The Gap**: Lack of accessible, private, and stigma-free tools for daily emotional well-being.

## Slide 2: Our Solution — Placida
- A human-centered mental wellness web app built on design thinking principles.
- **Zero Data Collection**: All data stays private in the user's browser (localStorage).
- **Core Features**: Mood logging, guided breathing, weekly reflection, AI emotional support, and relaxation games.

## Slide 3: Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: HTML5, CSS3 (Glassmorphism), Vanilla JS
- **Backend/Auth**: Supabase
- **Data Visualization**: Chart.js
- **Audio/Interactivity**: Web Audio API

## Slide 4: Live Demo Flow
1. **Home**: Landing page & Mood Logger
2. **Dashboard**: Personal stats and mood trends
3. **Breathe**: Guided 4-7-8 breathing exercises
4. **Chat**: AI emotional support with crisis detection
5. **Relax**: Stress-relief mini-games (Bubble Pop, Zen Canvas, Sound Tiles)
6. **Feedback**: In-app feedback collection

## Slide 5: User Feedback Results
*(Based on our Week 4 Feedback Analysis)*
- **High Praise**: Breathing exercises (4.5/5), Mood Logger (4.2/5), and calming aesthetics.
- **Areas for Improvement**: AI Chat needs deeper context handling; users want more historical dashboard stats.
- **NPS Score**: 7.4/10 ("Likely to recommend").
